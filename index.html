About Me

I am a researcher in the Department of Physics and the Weinberg Institute for Theoretical Physics at The University of Texas at Austin, focusing on gravitational-wave astrophysics, compact binary coalescences, and parameter estimation ($h(t) = F_+ h_+ + F_\times h_\times$).

My work explores how gravitational-wave observations, normalizing flows, and theoretical modeling reveal the astrophysics of compact objects and strong-field gravity.

Research Interests

Compact Binary Parameter Estimation & Detectability

Modeling spin-dependent detectability, selection effects, and Bayesian inference in gravitational-wave astronomy using normalizing flows and waveform models.

Dark Matter Spikes, Modified Gravity & Cosmology

Investigating compact binary merger rates in dark matter spikes under modified gravity, as well as 21-cm cosmology from the Epoch of Reionization.
