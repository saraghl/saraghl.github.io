About Me

I am an astronomy student focusing on compact binary coalescences, gravitational-wave data analysis, and parameter estimation ($h(t) = F_+ h_+ + F_\times h_\times$).

My work explores how next-generation detectors and Bayesian inference pipelines extract astrophysical population properties from binary black hole and neutron star mergers.

Research Interests

Compact Binary Parameter Estimation & Waveforms

Investigating binary black hole (BBH) and neutron star mergers using Bayesian inference, matched filtering, and numerical relativity surrogate models.

Detector Characterization & Noise Modeling

Analyzing non-stationary noise transients (glitches) and power spectral density estimation in ground-based interferometers (LIGO/Virgo/KAGRA) and space-based missions (LISA).
